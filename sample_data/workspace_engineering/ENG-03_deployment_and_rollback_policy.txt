# Production Deployment Strategies & Automated Rollback Policy
Document ID: ENG-03
Workspace: Engineering & Operations
Version: 2.8 | Last Updated: October 2026

## Section 1: Blue-Green Deployment Standards
All production deployments to our Kubernetes clusters adhere to the Blue-Green release paradigm:
- Blue Environment: Currently serving 100% of live production traffic.
- Green Environment: New target release version deployed and validated via automated canary tests.
- Validation Window: The Green environment runs synthetic health assertions for 10 minutes prior to receiving production traffic.

## Section 2: Automated Rollback Trigger Criteria
The deployment pipeline continuously monitors application telemetry. An automated rollback is triggered immediately if any of the following occur during the canary or cutover phase:
1. HTTP 5xx error rate exceeds 1.5% over any consecutive 3-minute evaluation window.
2. 95th percentile latency (p95) increases by more than 40% compared to baseline.
3. Health check endpoints fail on more than 20% of container pods.

## Section 3: Rollback Policy and Load Balancer Interaction
When a rollback is triggered (either automatically by telemetry alerts or manually during an active incident):
- Traffic Pointer Switch: The Application Load Balancer (ALB) instantly redirects the 100% traffic weight pointer back to the previous stable Blue environment.
- Connection Draining: The load balancer enforces a 30-second connection draining period on the failed Green cluster, stopping new HTTP requests while allowing in-flight requests to terminate.
- Emergency Override Authority: If a P1 incident is declared during a live deployment, the designated Incident Commander has unilateral authority to initiate an immediate hard-cut rollback, bypassing normal drain timers to prevent further service degradation.
