## Section 1: Executive Equity Vesting Guidelines
Standard executive equity grants are issued under the 2024 Long-Term Incentive Plan (LTIP):
- Vesting Schedule: Four-year standard vesting schedule with a mandatory one-year cliff (25% vests at 12 months), followed by monthly ratable vesting over the remaining 36 months.
- Acceleration Triggers: Single-trigger acceleration applies in the event of an involuntary termination without cause following a change-of-control transaction (e.g. merger or full company sale).

## Section 2: Executive Performance Bonus Milestones
Annual executive bonuses are tied directly to corporate KPI achievement:
- Threshold Tier (80% bonus payout): Requires meeting minimum ARR threshold of $70M.
- Target Tier (100% bonus payout): Requires meeting ARR of $74M and EBITDA margin > 18%.
- Stretch Tier (150% bonus payout): Requires exceeding $80M in ARR and closing at least one strategic corporate acquisition.
